Don’t Run Ads Yet: The 7-Point Landing Page Audit

Seven critical optimizations that convert paid clicks into qualified business leads instead of wasted ad spend.

Before you spend even ₹500 on Google Ads or Meta Ads, remember this ironclad law of digital marketing: great ads cannot rescue a broken landing page. If your page takes 6 seconds to load or lacks trust proofs, 80% of your paid traffic will vanish before reading your offer.

The 7-Point Landing Page Checklist

  1. Sub-2.5s Mobile Load Speed: Optimize images to modern WebP, defer non-critical JavaScript, and verify with Google PageSpeed Insights.
  2. Direct Headline Match (Ad-to-Page Continuity): If your ad promises “20% Off Tax Filing”, your headline must repeat that exact promise within 3 seconds of opening.
  3. Frictionless 3-Field Lead Form: Never ask for 10 fields on first contact. Name, Phone / WhatsApp, and Requirement are all you need to initiate conversation.
  4. 1-Click WhatsApp Instant Chat: Indian consumers prefer chatting on WhatsApp before filling forms. A floating WhatsApp widget increases lead volume by 35%+.
  5. Credibility & Local Social Proof: Client logos, real Google Maps review badges, testimonials, and company registration details.
  6. Single Clear Call-To-Action (No Distractions): Strip out top navigational menus, social media links that pull visitors away, or external links.
  7. Meta Pixel & Google Tag Manager Verified: Test conversion events using Meta Pixel Helper and Google Tag Assistant before turning campaigns live.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why do paid ads fail even with high click-through rates (CTR)? +

A high CTR proves your ad copy and creative are working, but a low conversion rate means your landing page fails to deliver on the ad promise, loads too slowly, or asks for too much commitment upfront.

What is the ideal page load speed for ad landing pages? +

Your landing page should load in under 2.5 seconds on a mobile 4G connection. Every additional 1 second of loading delay causes up to a 20% drop in ad conversions.

Should an ad landing page have the normal site navigation header? +

No. High-converting dedicated ad landing pages remove external header navigation links so visitors focus solely on the offer without leaking traffic to other pages.
